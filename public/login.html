The problem

Two people are stuck, and it is the same gap from both sides.

A home baker who sells on Instagram

She has captions and a WhatsApp price list, not a catalog or a checkout. To an AI shopping agent, her shop does not exist.

AgentReady reads her text, builds the catalog, and tells her why agents did not buy.

A buyer who wants an agent to handle it

Nothing stops an agent from overspending, or from paying a shop whose page quietly tells AI agents to trust it.

AgentReady enforces the buyer's limits in code and keeps the money on hold at PayPal until delivery.

How it works

The AI chooses. Code decides. PayPal holds the money.

01The agent finds it

Claude searches every agent-ready shop with tools, or your own assistant does over MCP.

02The guard checks it

Ten rules in plain code: your limits, shop trust, refund policy, scam wording. The model cannot override them.

03You approve on PayPal

The payment is authorized, not captured: intent: AUTHORIZE. The guard re-checks before the hold.

04Delivered, then paid

The shop marks it delivered and the hold is captured. Otherwise it is voided. Refunds go back through PayPal.

Under the hood

What is actually wired up.

PayPal Orders v2Authorize on approval, capture on delivery, void to release, refund after capture. Payee set per shop so money goes to the merchant.
PayPal webhooksSignature-verified. An approval or a dispute still lands if the buyer closes the tab; a dispute lowers that shop's trust for its next sale.
PayPal Agent ToolkitPublished products are registered in PayPal's catalog, and the shop owner's money assistant reads PayPal records. Read-only for the model.
ClaudeTool-use loops for the shopper, strict-schema catalog extraction, and the money assistant. Shop text is treated as data, never as instructions.
MCP endpointAny MCP client can search, dry-run the guard and check out at /mcp, under each buyer's own key and rules.
AG GridOrders ledger with quick filter, totals row and CSV export; an editable live catalog; the audit trail; lost sales.
RenderDeployed from render.yaml with a health check. Without keys it runs a PayPal simulator through the same code paths.
Tests44 automated tests on the guard, accounts, MCP checkout, webhooks and stale approvals, run by CI on Node 22 and 24.
